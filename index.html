How I work

Debugging is most of the job

Features matter, but the skill that grew fastest was finding out why a live system is behaving the way it is.

Root cause, not symptom

When something breaks in production I trace it end to end — logs, Kafka offsets, database rows — and confirm the cause with evidence before changing code.

Verify in the real system

A fix isn't finished when it compiles. I check the actual outcome in the database and the service logs after deploying, so I know the behaviour genuinely changed.

Follow the existing patterns

In a codebase of many services, I read how the neighbouring service solved the same problem before inventing a new approach — consistency keeps a platform maintainable.
